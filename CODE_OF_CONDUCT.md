# Code of Conduct

FloodWatch Nigeria is built to help people stay safe. We want the community around it to be welcoming, respectful and focused
on that goal.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people for whom English is a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Be honest and precise. This project warns people about danger, so unverified claims and overstated confidence are taken seriously.
- Respect privacy. Never post other people's phone numbers, locations or personal data in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexual or violent language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and
deliberately spreading false flood or heat information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

## Reporting a concern

Use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (the *Security* tab, then *Report a vulnerability*) and mark it as a conduct
concern, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and when someone is representing the project in public.
